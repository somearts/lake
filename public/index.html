    Everything Inside Lake

    All the features a good money app needs, each one made to feel calm for two people.

    Shared GoalsCreate a lake for every dream, from Kyoto to a first home or a baby, and fill it together.
    RipplesEvery deposit lands as a gentle ripple with a small note, so saving feels like a shared moment.
    Yours And OursKeep private accounts and a shared lake side by side, with each of you choosing what the other sees.
    Auto SavingSet a monthly flow into each lake and pause it whenever you like, without being asked why.
    Quiet DatesSee when a goal will arrive at your own pace, as a date to look forward to rather than a deadline to fear.
    Gentle RemindersSoft nudges for bills and goals, never more than you ask for.
    Private And SecureFace ID, encrypted data and read-only bank connections keep everything safe between the two of you.
    MilestonesSmall celebrations at halfway, almost there and full, arriving only when they are earned.

    Left Out On Purpose

    Most money apps work by making you feel watched. Here is what we chose not to build.

    Spending ScoresNobody is graded on how they spend, and nothing here turns a coffee into a verdict.
    Overspend AlertsNo red warnings and no lectures, just a quieter way to stay aware.
    Streak GuiltMiss a month and nothing breaks. Your lake simply waits for you.
    LeaderboardsNobody is ranked, not even the two of you, because this was never a competition.
